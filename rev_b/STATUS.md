  Belton sockets exactly rev A's (outline chassis x 53.27-278.70, y 103.98-175.73).

  (2 oz option intended, as the preamp; 1 oz also passes the rules), In1.Cu / In2.Cu inner, used as
  **low-voltage signal layers only, no plane** (the preamp's decision: a plane's picofarads on the
  high-impedance nodes; the outer GND pours keep 1 mm from them and are cut under them on the other
  layers). HV and MV copper is on the outer layers only, except where the HV leftover step had to use an

  with the HV spacing (about 75 % of the board committed before routing, the anneal never reached a
  legal placement); with the low-voltage 0603 parts on B (L5) it places, but on 2 layers the logic
  island (MCP23S17 SSOP-28, 3x DG412, ULN2803A, 2x LVC1G11, their 40 passives) left 8-37 logic / supply
  connections open in every ordering tried (the supply nets walled the expander in, or the logic nets
  walled the supplies out). With two inner layers for the low-voltage nets the same placement routes.
- JLCPCB capability (read 2026-11-24, https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities): 2 oz outer
  min track / space 0.16 / 0.16 mm (here 0.2 / 0.2 minimum, 0.3 default); via 0.8 / 0.3 mm (2 oz
  annular 0.25 mm); thermal vias in the DPAK tabs 0.6 / 0.3 mm; copper to routed edge >= 0.2 mm (here

### L4. How it was placed and routed (re-runnable)

1. `tools/make_pcb.py` (kipy): the board from the schematic netlist; outline, holes and sockets from
   rev A; every connector at its agreed pin 1 (`layout/placement.py` = interfaces.md 12.5); the two pass
   devices, their foldback transistors and the big capacitors pinned; net classes and rules.
2. `layout/place.sh`: by default the committed placement (`layout/placement_snapshot.json`); with
   `ANNEAL=1` / `SEEDS=...` KiCadRoutingTools `place_simple` (groups and keep-outs from
   `make_groups.py`; seed 4 of 1-9, halo 0.3, was the one kept), then `fix_place.py`: a short list of
   nudges with reasons, an HV edge push and a class-clearance push (pads of different parts closer than
   HV-LV 2.5 / HV-MV 1.25 / HV-HV 0.6 mm, HV pads near the standoffs), each followed by KRT's legaliser.
3. `layout/route_chain.sh` (kicad-agentic-routing pattern, one KRT step per net group, each locked
   before the next): 0 heater pair (`draw_heater.py`, scripted) and the drain pours with thermal vias
   (`draw_pours.py`); 1 HV and 2 MV (outer layers only); 3 HIZ; 4 audio; 5 supply (+5 V 0.5 mm,
   ±15 V 0.4 mm, GNDD 0.5 mm); 6 logic; 7 relay coils; 8 JFET gates; 9 leftovers with the logic, gate,
   coil, supply and audio nets unlocked for rip-up; 10 HV leftovers (inner layers allowed);
   11 GND pours on F and B (KRT `route_planes`, stitched); 12 island repair, HIZ cuts, refill
   (`finalize.py`); 13 GND tracks to the pads the pours could not reach. About 25 minutes.
4. Checks: `layout/drc.sh` (kicad-cli DRC with the project rules, zones refilled, schematic parity),
   `layout/mech_check.py`, `layout/coupling_check.py`, `layout/render.sh`.

Scripted copper (not the router): the heater pair, the drain pours with their thermal vias, the
expander's GPA0-GPA2 escapes (every routing order boxed one of them in). Everything else is KRT, in the
order above; HV and MV on the outer layers except about 57 mm of the regulator nets and BPI_P that the HV
step put on an inner layer (the class rules apply there too). Iterations that mattered: 2 layers left
8-37 logic / supply opens (-> 4 layers); the drain pours were eaten by the GND pours (KRT gives them
priority 21: the drain pours are now 40) and chopped by other nets' tracks (-> no tracks on the B
spreaders); the router does not apply class-scoped hole rules (-> track keep-outs round the standoffs).

### L5. Parts on the plate side (B): needs the user's OK

The rev A outline does not hold all 252 parts on the component side with the HV spacing. **78 parts
are on B**, the side that faces the plate (13.4 mm to the plate, the sockets and solder joints were the
only things there): the logic island's 38 0603 passives (pull-downs, gate pulls, decoupling, the DGND
soft tie R401 / C401), the 36 0603 parts of the JFET gate RC networks (mixer, pad, feedback T-switches),
and the B+FX zener pair D506 / D507 (SMA, 150 V / 180 V, under C504's body). All are <= 2.3 mm tall.
Consequences: a second SMT side at assembly (JLC double-sided assembly); D506 / D507 put MV / HV
copper on the plate side, 13.4 mm from the plate (the HV supplies' rule is >= 5 mm). The alternative
is a larger outline (not possible: the board reuses the chassis holes) or fewer parts (resistor arrays
for the 24 pull-downs would free about 1.5 cm², not enough on its own).

### L8. Signal integrity checks

`layout/coupling_check.py` (same or adjacent layer, within 2 mm), aggressors = the PI plates, the return
plate, the speaker-level feedback lines, SEND_SRC, the send follower, the logic and coil lines; victims =
the input, FX return, mixer, attenuator and PI grids:
- **None** for the PI plates (DRV_A / DRV_B), the speaker lines (SPK_x, FB_8R) or SEND_SRC -> RETURN_SIG:
  no loop around the power section and no W23 dry leak on the board.
- K1A (send follower cathode, full preamp level, about 1 kΩ) runs 33 mm beside G1B (return grid, fed
  from the mixer through 1k) on an adjacent layer, crossing it: a dry leak around the FX loop in series
  mode, estimated about -60 dB at 10 kHz (0.3 pF against about 50 kΩ): at the R5 target, **to reroute** in
  the next pass. K1A beside G1A / SB is the follower's own bootstrapped grid (in phase, harmless).
- P1B beside G1B (16 mm, 0.36 mm, adjacent layer): the return stage's own plate-grid capacitance (about
  0.2 pF; with the stage gain a corner near 1 MHz). RLY_ATT1_N beside ATT3 / ATT4: a coil drive edge
  only when the relay switches (inside the mute).
- Input: J101 to V1 pin 2 about 15 mm; the heater pair 15 mm or more from every grid pin.

### L9. Regulator pours and the thermal foldback

Each pass device (STD4N80K5 DPAK) has its own BP_RAW pour: on F round the tab (the MMBTA42 in a cut-out
of it), on B a spreader under the regulator (B+FX's reaches J501's raw tab), joined by 16 thermal vias in
each tab (0.3 mm; filled and capped on the order, IPC-4761 type VII, or accept some wicking). Other nets'
tracks are kept off the B spreaders. **As filled: B+FX F 0.8 + B 5.7 cm², B+PI F 1.5 + B 4.2 cm²** (2 oz),
about 6.5 / 5.7 cm² per device, i.e. at the 30-50 K/W the thermal simulation assumed
(`sim/preamp_supply/results/thermal_foldback.md`: at 70 °C a steady short settles at 0.5-0.7 W, pass
junction 93-97 °C). Re-runs for the spread of the estimate: 15-25 K/W (`thermal_foldback_layout.md`):
0.75-0.99 W, junction 87-92 °C; 60-100 K/W (`thermal_foldback_layout_small.md`): junction 98-99 °C at
60 K/W, 130-139 °C at 80-100 K/W (B+FX), so the spreaders must stay. Normal limits 16-19 mA against about
3 mA loads and start-up are fine across 15-100 K/W.

## Missed items, ranked

1. **Open-load LF stability (SAFE-5 / PI-3)**: 23-28° / 8 dB with the decided 47n caps (P6). Also
   the open-load large-signal case is not simulated at all: full drive into no load gives the
   highest plate voltages (OT flyback) and the feedback loop gain jumps to 26-28 dB.
2. **W23 send-to-return coupling** (P1, P2): with the IF-1.1 pinout the dry leak around the effects
   unit can be -23 to -53 dB at 10 kHz; and PAD_A as wired is a high-impedance audio node.
3. **The overall gain from the preamp output to the PI input is not specified anywhere.** The
   preamp delivers up to 20 V peak and the PI must stay under 18 V at the 0 dB attenuator step, so
   the loop needs ≤ -1 dB; drawn at -3.8 dB (requirement R1).
4. **Grid leak 100k (interfaces.md) vs 220k (pi_nfb)** and the **W24 DC level / cap rating** (P5,
   row 32).
5. **Mechanics of the kept outline:** the boards hang 15 mm under the plate with the component
   side toward it, so 47 µF / 450 V electrolytics (Ø16-18 mm), the 2.2 µF / 630 V film caps,
   the 5 W zeners and any heatsink do not fit standing on that side: they go on the other side
   (66 mm free below) or lie down. Rev A had the noval sockets on the bottom side: which side faces
   the plate in rev B, and where the tubes point, needs the user's confirmation. 235 parts plus
   the HV parts in 162 cm² is plausible with SMD but not checked (no layout in this session).
6. **Regulator heatsinking:** a shorted B+ output dissipates 3.5-4.3 W in the pass device
   indefinitely (the 100 mA fuse doesn't blow at the 8-10 mA foldback current). Proposed: insulated
   TO-220F clamped to the chassis plate through a 15 mm aluminium spacer, or a lower foldback.
7. **No RETURN-jack detect:** in series mode with nothing plugged into RETURN the amp goes silent.
   No pin or bit for a jack switch (requirement R6).
8. **DC on the effects unit's output** reaches the rear RETURN pot (scratchy pot); this board
   blocks it (220n) but the rear I/O should AC-couple at the jack.
9. **Send at full into a 10 kohm line input clips** (1.2 % at 9 V preamp, 15 % at 17 V): the
   follower can't drive 10 V peak into 11 kohm. Fine below about 6 V peak at the jack; the send pot
   at full is +16 dBu, beyond any line input. Documented, not fixed (requirement R3).
10. **Unplugged W25 (feedback lead):** the amp runs open loop (about 6 dB louder, more distortion),
    undetected (requirement R11).
11. **CCS spread:** DN2540 Vgs(off) 1.5-3.5 V moves the tail current 2:1 with a fixed source
    resistor: select or trim; the real-model run is missing.
12. **Heater pass-through current** (1.5 A on J502) and the 430 V creepage on a board that also
    carries ribbons: no requirement states either (R15, R17).
